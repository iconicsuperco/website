## Launch decisions

- Free standard delivery from ₹500; ₹49 below the threshold.
- Draft 7-day return/replacement flow, with damage or wrong-item reporting
  within 48 hours.
- Razorpay for online payments and Shiprocket for courier allocation/tracking.
- Weights and packed dimensions are intentionally not guessed. Those fields
  remain a pre-launch dependency on the seller CSV.
